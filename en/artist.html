ARTIST

YOUME Yumi

A national-treasure emotional vocalist spanning OST and live stages

Vocalist Yumi left a powerful impression on the public with her mega-hit debut song 'Love Is Always Thirsty' (사랑은 언제나 목마르다). With explosive vocal power and delicate emotional expression, she has carried the OSTs of countless acclaimed dramas and films. After proving her unrivaled vocals once again through 〈Sing Again〉 and 〈King of Mask Singer〉, she made a strong impact on 〈Miss Trot 4〉, finishing in the final TOP 10 — rallying an overwhelming fandom that reaches beyond ballad fans to middle-aged audiences, and continuing her second prime.
