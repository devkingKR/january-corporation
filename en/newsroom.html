Stress Solution & January Corporation Sign MOU — Reporter Kang Jin-hee, January 23, 2026. AI digital healthcare company Stress Solution (CEO Bae Ik-ryeol) announced it signed an MOU with broadcast content producer January Corporation (CEO Kim Jun-hee) on January 22 for joint development of an AI-based entertainment platform. The agreement targets a new sports entertainment program called 'Bowling Avengers,' applying Stress Solution's biometric data analysis and AI sound solution technology to create a differentiated viewing experience. The two companies plan to develop a format that extends content beyond match results to include participants' physical and mental condition. Stress Solution will provide heart rate and ECG-based analysis of tension, immersion, and physical flow, integrating its personalized sound solution 'HealingBeat' for stress management. The collaboration grew from a partnership formed at the Gangnam Startup & Employment Center. Stress Solution, which showcased its technology at CES 2026, aims to expand its healthcare expertise into the entertainment industry.

[EXCLUSIVE] Kim Tae-won, Kim Jong-seo, Kim Kyung-ho, Park Wan-kyu — 'Longhair Legend' debuts on KBS in September

Published: 2025-07-26 10:21:11 Updated: 2025-07-26 10:37:58

 

'Combined age 225, 140 years of career' — four legendary rock icons
Special MZ Generation guests announced

 

The four legendary rockers Kim Tae-won, Kim Jong-seo, Kim Kyung-ho, and Park Wan-kyu are coming together.  

 

According to Sports World reporting on the 26th, KBS's new real music variety show 'Longhair Legend' (producer: January Corporation) is set to premiere in early September.

 

'Combined age 225, combined career 140 years' — the four will embark on their first group trip through 'Longhair Legend,' weaving a story of cross-generational empathy and adventure. 

 

 

The four, known for their overwhelming stage charisma, will show a charming goofy side off stage with lively banter. The 'youngest' duo Kim Kyung-ho and Park Wan-kyu add extra fun with their 'Tom & Jerry' chemistry. 

 

Special guests also join the journey. 'MZgenie (MZ+Genie),' representing the MZ Generation, guides the rockers through the unfamiliar world of trending snacks, challenges, and social media culture. 

 

The 'Pyeongsang Live' segments and Kim Tae-won's advice corner add musical emotion and healing moments for viewers. 

 

The new music variety show ‘Longhair Legend’ premieres on KBS2TV in early September. Combining laughter, growth, and heartfelt moments from four legendary rockers, the program promises a new era of cross-generational music entertainment.

Yeoju City Hosts 'Picnic Live Sopung' Concert

[여주=팔당유역신문] 여주시가 오는 10월 2일 오후 6시에 여주 남한강 출렁다리 북단 잔디광장에서 MBC플러스 음악 프로그램 ‘피크닉 라이브 소풍’ 공연을 진행한다.

The concert is a 'healing busking' event held in nature outside a studio, allowing citizens to enjoy live performances against the backdrop of the Namhan River on an autumn evening.

Performers include singers Lee Mu-jin, Na Yun-kwon, Oh My Girl Yubin, and Yoo Sung-eun, with main filming starting at 6 PM. The broadcast will air nationwide on MBC Plus on October 24 at 6 PM.

Yeoju City expects the concert to: promote the Namhan River Suspension Bridge as a key tourism landmark, provide citizens with a live cultural experience, and boost Yeoju's tourism image through online clip sharing.

Yeoju City prepared the concert not just as a broadcast but as an autumn night music picnic for everyone. Admission is free and open to all.

Mayor Lee Chung-woo of Yeoju said: "It is very meaningful that the media facade of Yeoju Namhan River Suspension Bridge can accompany music to offer citizens a special autumn evening." “Picnic Live Sopung is a time to set down daily life amidst Yeoju's nature and share the leisure of autumn together.

He added: "We will continue to grow Yeoju as a city of culture and tourism that brightens citizens' lives."
